## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: professionals and students applying for jobs, internships, scholarships or promotions — often across borders (US/Canada, EU, GCC/Middle East, Asia-Pacific, academia) — who need a resume that both parses through applicant tracking systems and reads well to a human skimming in seconds.
Secondary: visitors evaluating FALO Enterprise itself (partners, future users of later FALO products).

## Product Purpose
FALO Enterprise is a product studio. Its flagship product is **ResuMax** (resumax.io, app at https://resumax-frontend.fly.dev/en), a resume/CV builder. This landing page introduces FALO and showcases ResuMax; success = the visitor understands what ResuMax does differently and opens the app.

## Positioning
ResuMax builds resumes around the standards recruiters and screening software actually use, formatted per region (the same profile rendered to US, Europass, GCC, or academic convention), with native Arabic RTL, an ATS check that shows exactly which checks pass and fail, and an AI writer bound by explicit refusals (it never invents achievements, never pads, never runs unless asked).

## Operating Context
Flow (verified from product): Answer 7 questions → layout picked and first draft filled → 6 guided sections (contact, summary, skills, experience, projects, education; drag to reorder, autosave) → Tailor for one of 7 purposes (job, internship, scholarship, promotion…) → Export as PDF or JPG after adjusting template, accent colour, font, and scale.
AI writing via Google Gemini enterprise API, only on button press; content not used for training. Payments via Paddle. Sign-in with email, Google, GitHub.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 6 template families: Executive Teal (Finance/Mgmt), Developer Sidebar (Tech/STEM), Creative Director (Design/Art), Classic ATS (Business/General, free), Academic CV (Education/Research), Global Professional (Global/Remote).
- ATS optimizer with keyword analysis and named pass/fail checks; real-time preview; multi-region formats; English + Arabic RTL.
- Cover Letter Builder: marked "Soon" on the product home — present as upcoming.
- Product version: 1.0, "launching soon"/early.
- **Undecided / inconsistent on the live site — do not state on this page:** pricing figures ($9 vs $9.99), template counts (6 vs 120+), free-plan limits, DOCX/LinkedIn export.
- Primary CTA: open the ResuMax app (https://resumax-frontend.fly.dev/en; signup at /en/createaccount).
- This landing page: English only.

## Evidence on Hand
- Product principles ("Five things we will never do"): don't invent achievements; don't pad to fill a page; don't track you (no analytics/ad pixels/third-party cookies); don't send anything to AI unless asked; don't keep what you delete.
- Cited stat used by the product: "88% of employers say qualified candidates get filtered out because their wording didn't match the job description." — Harvard Business School & Accenture, Hidden Workers, 2021.
- Template preview images: imgur URLs from the product's templates page.
- **Absent — do not fabricate:** user counts, testimonials (those on the product site are unverified), customer logos, pricing, satisfaction metrics. Prove through product demonstration with sample data labeled as sample.

## Product Principles
1. Demonstrate, don't claim — the mechanism on screen beats adjectives.
2. Honesty is the brand: no invented numbers, no guarantees ATS cannot honor.
3. Global by construction — regional conventions and RTL are first-class, not add-ons.
4. The user stays in control of their words and their data.
